Regional extracts: Geofabrik, https://download.geofabrik.de/asia/china.html
Per-area background files preserve the source PBF URI, retrieval time and hash.
Rail station evidence and source extract hashes: data/osm-station-provenance.json

Public station coordinates are derived from named OSM nodes and OSM route-member
track geometry. Unverified names and disconnected intervals are omitted.
No operator timetable or live train position is supplied by this database.

Official map images, precise traced operator drawings, AMap snapshots and
unverified timetable/catalogue bundles are not included in this public source
package. Their original authors' rights are not relicensed by this project.
